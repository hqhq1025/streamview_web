# Earn on YouTube

**URL**: https://studio.youtube.com/channel/UCtxQrYzeMBCKMAofCgcY5dA/monetization
**Depth**: 1

## Overview
The Earn page is the central hub for channel monetization in YouTube Studio. It displays the creator's current progress toward monetization milestones, shows available and upcoming earning features, and provides eligibility requirements for each monetization method. The page is designed to guide creators through their monetization journey with clear visual progress indicators and actionable next steps.

## Main Components

### 1. Next Milestone Section
- **Title**: "Start your earning journey"
- **Description**: Earn income by connecting with fans and gain access to support forums and creator tools
- **Badge**: Visual indicator showing this is the current milestone

### 2. Earning Methods (Next Milestone)
Three expandable/collapsible cards:

**Memberships** (expanded by default)
- Tagline: "Grow your community and earn monthly"
- Description: Excite audience with exclusive perks as monthly paying members
- Details: Viewers can join from channel and video pages; creator receives 70% of net revenue

**Supers**
- Tagline: "Strengthen connections with your fans"
- Description: Super Chat, Super Stickers, and Super Thanks for community engagement
- Details: Earn each time fans buy highlighted chat or animated messages

**Shopping**
- Tagline: "Connect your online store to YouTube"
- Description: Connect a store to promote products like merchandise on your channel

### 3. Eligibility Progress Section
Shows current channel metrics against monetization requirements:

**Next Milestone Requirements:**
- 500 subscribers (currently: 0)
- 3 video uploads in last 90 days (currently: 1)
- AND one of:
  - 3,000 valid public watch hours in last 365 days (currently: 0)
  - 3M valid public Shorts views in last 90 days (currently: 0)

**Data freshness note**: "Showing data as of: Dec 26, 2025" with tooltip explaining validation delay

**Progress tracking**: Visual progress bars for each metric

### 4. Notification Button
- "Get notified" button - when clicked, changes to "We'll let you know when you're eligible" and disables "Apply now" button
- Shows "All changes saved" confirmation

### 5. Future Milestone Section
- **Title**: "Continue to grow"
- **Description**: Unlock even more ways to earn—including ads on videos

**Earning Methods:**

**Watch Page Ads**
- Tagline: "Earn from ads & YouTube Premium on the Watch Page"
- Details: Earn from ads on/around videos and from YouTube Premium subscribers

**Shorts Feed Ads**
- Tagline: "Earn from ads & YouTube Premium in the Shorts Feed"
- Details: Earn from ads between Shorts and from Premium subscribers watching Shorts

**Future Milestone Requirements:**
- 1,000 subscribers (currently: 0)
- AND one of:
  - 4,000 valid public watch hours in last 365 days (currently: 0)
  - 10M valid public Shorts views in last 90 days (currently: 0)

### 6. Creator Resources & Tools Section
Links to helpful tools:
- YouTube Creator Support
- Copyright Match Tool
- Video end screens

### 7. Resources Section
Categorized links:

**Support:**
- Help Center (opens in new tab)
- Community forums

**Learn:**
- YouTube Creators Channel

**Things to know:**
- Channel monetization policies
- Advertiser-friendly guidelines
- Other ways to earn

### 8. After You Apply Section
- Explanation of review process for monetization policies
- "Learn more" button linking to detailed information

## Functionality

### Expandable/Collapsible Cards
- Each earning method can be expanded to show more details
- Only one card per section can be expanded at a time
- Smooth expand/collapse animations
- Visual indicators (arrow icons) show expand/collapse state

### Progress Tracking
- Real-time display of channel metrics
- Visual progress bars show progress toward each threshold
- Data is validated and may take up to a week to update

### Notification System
- "Get notified" button allows creators to receive alerts when eligible
- Button changes state after clicking
- Shows confirmation message "All changes saved"

### External Links
- Multiple support and resource links
- Open in new tabs to avoid losing place on page
- Cover policies, guidelines, and learning resources

## Interactive Elements

### Buttons
- "Get notified" - enables eligibility notifications
- "Apply now" - disabled until eligibility requirements are met
- "Learn more" - navigates to detailed monetization information
- Expand/Collapse buttons - show/hide earning method details

### Links
- Creator support resources
- Help Center and Community forums
- Policy and guideline documents
- Educational content

### Expandable Sections
- Memberships, Supers, Shopping (Next Milestone)
- Watch Page Ads, Shorts Feed Ads (Future Milestone)
- Each section shows title, tagline, and detailed description

## Notable Observations

### Design Patterns
- **Progressive disclosure**: Information is organized into expandable cards to reduce cognitive load
- **Visual hierarchy**: Clear distinction between current and future milestones with different styling
- **Motivational design**: Progress bars and milestone badges encourage creators to continue growing
- **Action-oriented**: Clear CTAs guide creators toward next steps

### Technical Details
- Data validation delay of up to one week is clearly communicated
- Metrics are validated differently than in YouTube Analytics
- Page uses real-time data updates with timestamps
- Accessibility features include ARIA roles and keyboard navigation

### User Experience
- **Clear goal setting**: Two distinct milestone paths (current and future) provide achievable targets
- **Transparency**: Detailed eligibility requirements help creators understand what's needed
- **Support integration**: Multiple help resources are readily accessible
- **Encouragement**: Positive messaging focuses on growth and opportunity

### Business Logic
- Monetization is tiered: lower thresholds for initial features, higher for ads
- Multiple paths to eligibility (watch hours OR Shorts views) provide flexibility
- Revenue sharing is clearly communicated (70% for memberships)
- Policy compliance is emphasized as part of the application process
